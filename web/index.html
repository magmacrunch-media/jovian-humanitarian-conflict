MUSIC
“The Jovian Humanitarian Conflict” by Jimmi, from the album JIMMI. The game takes its name and its subject from the track.
HOW TO PLAY
Fly with the arrows or WASD. Fire with Z or Space. Contacts come at you out of the vanishing point. Aid convoys squawk a double blink and carry a cross. Hostiles run dark and angular. The strip along the top shows every contact, including the ones you cannot pick out of the sky.

Saving a convoy means killing the hostile hunting it. When one is marked, a line joins it to its attacker, a ring marks that hostile, and a bar under the convoy shows how long you have. Shoot the ringed hostile before the bar empties.
SCORING
Hostile destroyed +100 × combo. Convoy escorted clear +500. A convoy lost to hostiles costs you the combo. Shooting one yourself costs 1,000 and one of three strikes. Three ends the run, whatever the score says.
